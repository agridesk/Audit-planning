AUDIT MANAGEMENT SYSTEM — ANNUAL PLANNER DATA CONTRACT
Date: 2026-09-26
Branch: dev
Status: DESIGN ONLY — no runtime behavior change
Authority: AMS_MASTER_ROADMAP_V2.8_FINAL_2026-09-26

1. PURPOSE
Annual Planner is a medium-term planning surface for concept month and Auditor preassignment.
It must help distribute future workload without prematurely creating concrete planning state.
It must also answer a practical management question early enough to act: do we have enough qualified audit capacity per month/period for the expected demand, or do we need to move concept work, rebalance auditors or scale capacity up?

2. HARD BOUNDARY
Annual Planner may store only planning intentions that do NOT yet create:
- Planning JSON;
- Availability occupancy;
- lifecycle status change;
- customer proposal;
- final Auditor acceptance.

Concrete date/time planning remains owned by Planning 2.0 Workspace / Batch flows.

3. CANONICAL INPUTS
Annual Planner reads:
- Audit_Obligations / planning demand;
- planning window / expiry context;
- Company Region;
- Preferred_Audit_Months;
- preassignment / Allow self planning policy;
- qualification;
- rotation;
- Auditor_Exclusions;
- Min_Interval_Months;
- completed history;
- committed and provisional workload;
- available/plannable Auditor capacity by month/period where available from canonical Availability/capacity sources.

4. CONCEPT FIELDS
Per Audit Obligation / future planning item:
- conceptMonth: optional YYYY-MM;
- preassignedAuditor: optional canonical Auditor identity;
- planningNote: optional operational note if needed;
- revision / updatedAt / updatedBy.

No concrete day/start/end belongs in Annual Planner concept state.

5. CONCEPT MONTH RULES
Concept month:
- is advisory planning intent;
- should normally intersect or be compatible with the planning window;
- should use Preferred_Audit_Months as a preference, not blindly as a hard rule;
- may remain empty;
- does not reserve capacity.

If concept month later becomes infeasible, the Planner should surface that fact instead of silently moving it.

6. PREASSIGNMENT RULES
Preassignment:
- is a strong preference;
- already subject to hard feasibility checks such as qualification, Auditor_Exclusions and rotation policy;
- does not equal final assignment;
- does not occupy Availability;
- can later be overridden by Manager when shared planning intelligence shows a materially better feasible alternative.

Override must remain visible/traceable.

7. WORKLOAD AND CAPACITY VIEW
Per Auditor and per relevant month/period show at least:
- committed workload;
- provisional workload;
- concept workload;
- total exposure;
- available/plannable capacity where canonically measurable;
- remaining capacity or shortfall.

Concept workload must remain visually distinct from provisional and committed workload.
Do not represent concept work as booked capacity.

The Annual Planner must also provide an aggregate month/period view across the planning team:
- expected/planning demand in hours and/or planning-duration units;
- available qualified capacity;
- remaining headroom;
- capacity shortfall;
- concentration risk where demand depends too heavily on one Auditor or a very small qualified pool.

The Manager must be able to make the practical medium-term decisions the Annual Planner exists for, for example:
- place one audit in May, another in August and another in September;
- see whether this distribution fits expected capacity;
- rebalance concept months or preassignments before concrete planning starts;
- identify early that extra Auditor capacity, external support or another operational scaling measure may be needed.

Capacity sufficiency is decision support, not an automatic staffing decision. The system surfaces the expected gap; the Manager decides how to respond.

8. FILTERS / DEFAULT VIEW
Default view prioritises upcoming/relevant planning windows.
Supported filters:
- month;
- Auditor;
- Region.

A blank Auditor/Region filter must remain valid and support planning-window-first distribution.

9. VALIDATION
On save of concept month or preassignment:
- validate current revision;
- reject stale overwrite;
- hard-block impossible Auditor preassignment;
- return soft warnings separately from hard blockers;
- do not call final planning writer.

Capacity overload at concept level should normally be a visible planning warning rather than silently blocking the Manager, unless a separate hard feasibility rule is violated.

10. TRANSITION TO PLANNING 2.0
When Manager opens a concrete planning flow:
- concept month and preassignment are input context only;
- current facts are re-read;
- current hard feasibility is recalculated;
- concrete planning can differ from concept after Manager decision;
- accepted concept is never treated as committed planning.

11. SHARED INTELLIGENCE
Annual Planner consumes the same shared planning intelligence as:
- single-audit planning;
- Batch/Workweek;
- replacement search;
- future suggestion surfaces.

No separate annual-planning scoring engine.

12. 3S + VALUE
Speed
- use bounded planning-demand/read models;
- fast filters and workload/capacity summaries;
- no broad per-row live eligibility scans on every interaction.

Scalability
- concept state is lightweight;
- aggregate workload/capacity read model per Auditor/month;
- dynamic scope handling;
- team-level monthly capacity summaries avoid N+1 Auditor calculations.

Stability
- optimistic concurrency;
- no accidental occupancy/lifecycle writes;
- hard preassignment feasibility fails closed;
- audit trail for concept changes;
- capacity summaries distinguish measured/known capacity from assumptions or unavailable data.

Value
- gives Manager early workload visibility;
- shows whether planned monthly distribution is likely to fit available capacity;
- provides early warning when Auditor capacity may need to be increased;
- supports future bulk planning;
- reduces last-minute planning without pretending future intentions are bookings.

13. SAFE STATUS
This document does not change:
- Manager Overview -> Plan route;
- Planning Workspace authentication/opening;
- lifecycle writes;
- Availability;
- Companies data;
- PROD.
